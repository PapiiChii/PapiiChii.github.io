About Me

ABOUT ME

Turning Complex Data Into Clear, Actionable Insights

I am an MSc Data Science graduate with a background in healthcare support and a strong interest in using data to improve services, reporting and decision-making.

My analytical experience includes working with large and complex datasets, data cleaning and validation, exploratory data analysis, statistical modelling, SQL database querying, Power BI reporting and machine learning.

For my MSc research at the University of Salford, I analysed UK population data involving 14,914 cases and developed an end-to-end analytical pipeline covering data preprocessing, missing-data assessment, feature engineering, model evaluation and explainable machine learning.

Alongside my technical background, I have practical experience working in health and social care. This has given me a strong understanding of confidentiality, accurate documentation, data quality and the importance of reliable information in supporting safe and effective services.

My technical toolkit includes SQL, Power BI, Excel, Python, Power Query and statistical analysis. I enjoy investigating data-quality issues, identifying trends and anomalies, and presenting findings in a way that helps people make informed decisions.

Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Data Modelling | Data Quality

The project included: Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query Relational data modelling DAX measures and calculated fields Data-quality checks and anomaly investigation Peak and off-peak performance analysis Interactive KPI and trend reporting Key outcome: Improved the reliability of the reporting logic and created a clearer view of transport activity across routes and operating periods.

Predictive Supply Chain Pipeline: From SQL Staging to Explainable ML

PostgreSQL | Python (pandas & scikit-learn) | SHAP | Random Forest

Implemented custom python feature engineering to calculate spatial volumes and consumer friction ratios.
