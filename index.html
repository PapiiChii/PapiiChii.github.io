About Me

Turning complex data into useful information

I am an MSc Data Science graduate with a background in healthcare support and a strong interest in using data to improve services, reporting and decision-making.

My analytical experience includes working with large and complex datasets, data cleaning and validation, exploratory data analysis, statistical modelling, SQL database querying, Power BI reporting and machine learning.

For my MSc research at the University of Salford, I analysed UK population data involving 14,914 cases. I developed an end-to-end analytical pipeline covering preprocessing, missing-data assessment, feature engineering, model evaluation and explainable machine learning.

Alongside my technical background, I have practical experience in health and social care. This has developed my understanding of confidentiality, accurate documentation, data quality and information governance .

I enjoy investigating data-quality issues, identifying trends and anomalies, and presenting findings in a way that supports informed decisions.
